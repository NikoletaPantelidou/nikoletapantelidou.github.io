About

I am a PhD student at the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona and an active member of the Centre de Lingüística Teòrica .

I am currently working on the MESSI project (Measuring Semantic Similarity in Bilingual Humans and Language Models) under the supervision of Prof. Evelina Leivada.

My research lies at the intersection of Natural Language Processing, Computational Linguistics, and cognitive approaches to language, with a particular focus on understanding the linguistic abilities of Large Language Models.

Research

Large Language Models

Understanding the linguistic behaviour and grammatical patterns of LLMs.

Computational Linguistics

Exploring language through computational and quantitative methods.

Language & Cognition

Connecting computational models of language with theories of human cognition.

Semantic Similarity

Comparing semantic representations in humans and language models.

Background

Before starting my PhD, I received my Master's Degree in Cognitive Science and Language from the Universitat de Barcelona.

Previously, I completed my Bachelor's Degree in English Language and Literature at the University of Cyprus.

I was awarded the International Student Award by the Republic of Cyprus for obtaining the highest entrance grade among international students in the School of Humanities.

I am currently supported by an FPI fellowship, a competitive predoctoral contract awarded for doctoral research by the Spanish Ministry of Science and Innovation.
